Meet Chef Jose

Food created with skill, care and purpose.

Chef Jose De Freitas is the founder of Paprika Culinary Creations, a Johannesburg-based culinary company specialising in personalised dining experiences.

From intimate anniversary dinners and weekly family meals to weddings, corporate lunches and milestone celebrations, every menu is individually planned to reflect the client, occasion and style of service.
